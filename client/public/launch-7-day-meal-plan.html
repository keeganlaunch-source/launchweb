🚀 Day 1: Launch Day

🌅 Breakfast: Power Launch Scramble

Ingredients:
  • 3 whole eggs
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1 slice whole grain toast
  • 1 tsp olive oil
Prep: Scramble eggs with spinach in olive oil. Serve with avocado and toast.
Nutrition
Calories: 420
Protein: 22g
Carbs: 25g
Fat: 28g

🥪 Lunch: Rocket Fuel Salad

Ingredients:
  • 150g grilled chicken breast
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil vinaigrette
  • 1/4 cup walnuts
Prep: Combine all ingredients. Toss with vinaigrette.
Nutrition
Calories: 485
Protein: 35g
Carbs: 12g
Fat: 35g

Nutrition
Calories: 520
Protein: 35g
Carbs: 35g
Fat: 25g

🎯 LAUNCH Success Tips

💧 Hydration Protocol: Drink 500ml water upon waking, then 250ml before each meal. Aim for clear/light yellow urine.
⏰ Timing Strategy: Eat every 3-4 hours. Never skip breakfast. Stop eating 3 hours before bed.
🥘 Meal Prep Magic: Sunday prep saves the week. Cook proteins in bulk, pre-cut vegetables, portion snacks.
📏 Portion Guide: Palm-sized protein, fist-sized vegetables, cupped-hand carbs, thumb-sized fats.
🔄 Substitution Rules: Can't find an ingredient? Swap similar proteins, any green vegetable works, choose complex carbs over simple.
📱 Track Progress: Take progress photos, measure waist circumference, note energy levels daily.
